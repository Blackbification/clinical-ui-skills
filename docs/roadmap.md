# Roadmap

## v0.1 — complete public foundation

- 12 Agent Skills;
- 72 synthetic evals across 10 categories;
- Clinical Slop Catalog;
- Clinical UI Score + AI Slop Score;
- ClinicalUIBench schema/scoring/protocol;
- stdlib-only installer and validation tooling;
- heuristic frontend linter;
- three reproducible before/after demos;
- CI/tests/community templates;
- MIT licensing and public evidence map.

## v0.2 — automation and adapters

- browser/screenshot audit harness;
- optional axe-core or equivalent accessibility adapter;
- React/Tailwind/shadcn and component-library integration examples;
- machine-readable audit output schema;
- richer screenshot/state capture across desktop/tablet/mobile;
- community ClinicalUIBench submissions and public leaderboard process;
- additional workflows: messaging, handoff and medication/result review.

## v0.3 — benchmark maturity

- multi-model controlled benchmark runs;
- blinded multi-reviewer scoring;
- inter-rater reliability reporting;
- explicit regression tracking between releases;
- design-system adapters (for example Carbon/OpenMRS or NHS-inspired public patterns without copying product branding);
- translated patient-facing eval subsets.

## v1.0 — stable methodology

Target only after real public use: stable skill names/contracts, versioned benchmark methodology, documented migration policy and a mature contributor/reviewer process.
