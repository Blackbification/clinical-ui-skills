# Evidence and source policy

Clinical UI Skills combines formal guidance, open healthcare software patterns, accessibility standards and project-specific heuristics. These sources do not have equal evidentiary status.

## Primary public sources

- Agent Skills specification: https://agentskills.io/specification
- FDA Human Factors and Medical Devices: https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/human-factors-and-medical-devices
- FDA Human Factors Considerations: https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/human-factors-and-medical-devices/human-factors-considerations
- FDA Applying Human Factors and Usability Engineering to Medical Devices: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/applying-human-factors-and-usability-engineering-medical-devices
- NIST Technical Basis for User Interface Design of Health IT: https://www.nist.gov/publications/technical-basis-user-interface-design-health-it
- NIST Health Information Technology Usability: https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/health-information-technology-usability
- ONC SAFER Guides: https://healthit.gov/clinical-quality-and-safety/safer-guides/

- OpenMRS Patient Chart: https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-esm-patient-chart

## Anti-slop prior art

- https://github.com/rwcod/anti-ai-slop-ui
- https://github.com/yuwen-lu/unslop-ui
- https://github.com/LucasSantana-Dev/repaint

Clinical UI Skills does not copy their text or visual assets. It adapts the broader ideas — intentional design, anti-generic review, linting and render/audit loops — to healthcare-specific frontend tasks.

## Evidence caution

The project separates: **normative requirement**, **public guidance**, **observed healthcare pattern**, and **project heuristic**. Do not cite a project heuristic as if it were regulatory law.

## Regulatory caution

The presence of a rule in this repository does not establish compliance with FDA, EU MDR/IVDR, IEC 62366-1, IEC 60601-1-6, ISO 14971, EN 301 549, local health-IT regulation or any other framework. Products must determine their own applicable requirements.
