# Community Code of Conduct

Clinical UI Skills welcomes rigorous disagreement about interface design, evidence, accessibility, human factors and clinical workflows. Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and in good faith;
- distinguish evidence from opinion and project heuristics;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory conduct;
- protect patient privacy and never post PHI/PII;
- avoid sharing confidential employer, customer or health-system material without authorization;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they materially affect a contribution;
- accept that maintainers may edit, close or remove content that creates safety, privacy, legal or community risks.

Serious conduct or security concerns should be reported through the repository's available private reporting channel when appropriate.

Maintainers may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, patients, users or the integrity of the project.
